Support

Need help with CaliCodex, found a problem, or have feedback?

Email: support@calicodex.app

When reporting a problem

It is helpful to include:

  • what you were trying to do;
  • what happened instead;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version, if relevant;
  • your CaliCodex app version; and
  • a screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

Local app data

CaliCodex stores training and progress data locally on your device and does not use a CaliCodex account or cloud account.

CaliCodex support does not have access to your locally stored training data and cannot recover it if the app and its local data are deleted.

Privacy

You can read the CaliCodex Privacy Policy for information about how the app handles data.
